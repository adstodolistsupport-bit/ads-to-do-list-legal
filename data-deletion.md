# Request Data Deletion — ADS To Do List

**App:** ADS To Do List (Android package: `com.ads.todolist`)  
**Developer:** ADS Digital Apps, Trinidad and Tobago  
**Contact:** [adstodolist.support@gmail.com](mailto:adstodolist.support@gmail.com)

You can use this page to **request deletion of personal information handled by ADS Digital Apps** even if you no longer have the app installed. You do not need to create or sign in to an ADS account.

## Request deletion

**[Request data deletion by email](mailto:adstodolist.support@gmail.com?subject=ADS%20To%20Do%20List%20-%20Data%20Deletion%20Request)**

The link opens your email application with the subject **ADS To Do List - Data Deletion Request**. To submit your request, write what you would like deleted and **send the email**. If the button does not open your email application, email **adstodolist.support@gmail.com** directly with that subject.

Please tell us whether your request concerns a support message, subscription-related information or other information you believe we hold. Include the email address you previously used to contact our support team if relevant. If additional information is necessary to locate a record or confirm that you are entitled to request deletion, we will ask for only what is reasonably needed.

**Do not send passwords, full payment-card numbers, sensitive task contents or unnecessary personal information.**

## What information can we review for deletion?

- **Support correspondence:** Messages, attachments and contact details you chose to send directly to ADS Digital Apps. We can review requests to delete records we control.
- **Subscription-related information:** Where applicable, we can review deletion of customer identifiers and related data processed for ADS Digital Apps through RevenueCat. We may need to verify that the request relates to the correct record before requesting or carrying out deletion.
- **Other information held on your behalf:** If you identify other personal information controlled by ADS Digital Apps, we will review your request and explain what action is possible.

Some information may need to be retained for legal obligations, recordkeeping, fraud prevention or the operation of an active subscription. Google Play independently controls some purchase and transaction records. We will explain any relevant limitation when handling your request.

## Tasks and notes stored on your phone

ADS To Do List **primarily stores tasks, notes, reminders, preferences and task attachments locally on your device**. ADS Digital Apps does not have a cloud account containing your task list and cannot remotely access or erase data that exists only on your phone.

You can delete tasks and other items using the app's available deletion controls. Where the app has a **Recently Deleted** area, use its permanent-delete option if you want to erase an item immediately. To remove the app's local data completely, you can clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. **Clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently erase information that cannot be recovered.** Copies in device backups or external services must be managed with those providers.

Calendar events, messages, exported files or attachments you sent to other apps or people are not removed by deleting local ADS To Do List data. Manage those copies with the corresponding provider or recipient.

## Subscriptions and deletion

**Deleting personal information or uninstalling ADS To Do List does not cancel an ADS Pro subscription.** If you have an active subscription, cancel it separately through [Google Play subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions).

A request involving subscription records may affect the ability to verify or restore previous purchases; we will explain any relevant consequences before an irreversible deletion.

## What happens next?

ADS Digital Apps will review the request, take reasonable steps to verify it when needed, and respond through email. We will delete eligible records we control or work with the relevant provider where appropriate, subject to applicable requirements, and explain if some records cannot be deleted. We do not promise that data maintained independently by Google Play or other third parties can be deleted by ADS Digital Apps.

This page is for **data deletion requests**, not ADS account deletion: this version of ADS To Do List does **not** offer registration or an ADS account login system.
