# Privacy Policy for ADS To Do List

**Effective Date: September 23, 2026**  
**Last Updated: October 12, 2026**

ADS Digital Apps ("we", "our", or "us"), based in Trinidad and Tobago, provides the ADS To Do List Android application ("the App"), package `com.ads.todolist`.

This policy explains how information is accessed, used, stored and disclosed when you use the App and our legal website. For privacy questions or requests, contact [adstodolist.support@gmail.com](mailto:adstodolist.support@gmail.com).

**At a glance:** Your task content is primarily stored on your device. This does not mean that every feature works entirely offline. Subscriptions use Google Play and RevenueCat; voice recognition may use your device's speech-service provider; and calendar, messaging, email and sharing features can pass information to services you choose.

## 1. Tasks and information on your device

Information you enter or generate may include task titles and descriptions, dates, times, reminders, repeat settings, priorities, categories, notes, checklists, completion records, productivity information, preferences, Assistant settings and selected attachments. Action Tasks may also contain phone numbers, email recipients, prepared messages, subjects and website addresses.

The App primarily stores this information locally in its application storage. It uses the information to display and organise your plans, prepare reminders, show progress and perform the features you request. Older installations may retain productivity records from earlier versions until you delete them.

This release does not provide an ADS Digital Apps login/account system or an ADS-operated cloud backup or task-sync service. Task content and attachments are not automatically uploaded to an ADS Digital Apps task-storage server. Information can leave your device through the optional features and subscription services explained below.

## 2. ADS Speak and microphone access

When you choose ADS Speak, the App requests microphone and speech-recognition access to turn your speech into proposed tasks. It displays recognised text and task details for you to review before saving. You can create tasks manually without using the microphone.

Recognition is handled through the speech-recognition service available on your Android device. Depending on the device, provider and settings, recognition may take place on-device or audio and recognition-related information may be sent to the provider's servers. The App does not require exclusively on-device recognition, so you should not assume that voice processing is always offline.

The App uses the resulting text to prepare tasks. The current ADS Speak implementation does not save an audio recording as a task attachment or upload audio to an ADS Digital Apps speech server. This does not determine whether the device's recognition provider processes or retains audio under its own practices. Review that provider's privacy information before dictating sensitive content.

You can revoke microphone permission in Android settings; voice entry will then be unavailable until permission is granted again.

## 3. ADS Assistant

ADS Assistant uses the requests you enter and relevant task information to prepare suggestions, find tasks and perform supported task actions you request. The App also uses locally stored Assistant preferences to personalise the experience. Review proposed task details and changes before confirming them.

The Assistant's use of task information does not create an ADS cloud task-backup account. Using ADS Speak to prepare text, or opening an external service from a task, is subject to the separate processing described in this policy.

## 4. Contacts and Action Tasks

When you tap Contacts for a supported Action Task, the App requests contact access and opens a contact picker. It can read the selected contact's name and phone numbers to let you select a destination. The chosen phone number is saved with the task locally. You can type a number manually instead of granting contact permission. The App does not upload your address book to an ADS Digital Apps server.

When you open a Call, WhatsApp, SMS, Email or Website action, the App passes the relevant destination and any prepared content to the dialer, messaging app, email app, browser or associated service. Opening a link or prepared action can disclose those details to that service even before you finish sending a message. WhatsApp actions, for example, use a WhatsApp link containing the selected number and any prepared message.

The App does not automatically send WhatsApp, SMS or email messages when a reminder becomes due. You review and send messages in the destination app. External services handle information under their own terms and privacy practices.

## 5. Calendar access and Calendar Sync

Calendar Sync requests calendar access when you choose to use it. The App uses that access to identify available calendars and create, update or remove calendar events associated with tasks. Information involved may include task titles, dates, times, task details and identifiers used to associate a task with a calendar or event.

Events written to a calendar are handled by the calendar app and account you select. Your calendar provider may synchronise them to its servers or other devices, depending on your account settings. That is different from an ADS-operated cloud task-sync service.

You can manage calendar permissions in Android settings and available Calendar Sync preferences in the App. Removing a task or turning off permission does not necessarily delete every external calendar copy; use the App's available event-deletion options or manage the event directly in your calendar.

## 6. Notifications, reminders and ringtones

The App uses task dates, times, reminder preferences and related identifiers to schedule device notifications. It requests notification permission and may require exact-alarm-related access for reminder features. Device services are also used for vibration, selected ringtone playback and reminder handling after device or app events.

Notification content can appear on your lock screen depending on your Android settings. You can control notification visibility, permissions and available alarm settings on your device. Selecting a ringtone uses Android's sound-selection features; it does not upload your ringtone library to ADS Digital Apps.

## 7. ADS Pro Suite, Google Play and RevenueCat

Optional ADS Pro Suite subscriptions are purchased and billed through Google Play. Google Play processes payment details; ADS Digital Apps does not receive your complete payment-card number through the App.

The App uses RevenueCat to load subscription offerings, verify entitlement status, restore eligible purchases and manage access to paid features. Subscription processing involves purchase history, product and transaction identifiers or purchase tokens, subscription status, an app/customer identifier and related technical information such as device type, operating system and app interaction timestamps needed by the subscription service.

RevenueCat processes subscription information on our behalf to provide subscription functionality and subscription analytics. ADS Digital Apps may access the associated customer and purchase records through its developer tools. This processing is separate from uploading task contents or your address book. RevenueCat documents that it encrypts data in transit using TLS.

See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [RevenueCat's privacy information](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). Those resources describe the providers' practices; they do not remove our responsibility for data processed for the App.

## 8. Attachments, exports and sharing

When you select a document or image for a task, the App may copy it into local application storage. It does not automatically upload task attachments to an ADS Digital Apps file server.

Opening, saving, exporting, emailing or sharing a task, report or attachment can pass the selected information to another application, storage provider or recipient. These transfers are initiated through the features you choose. Copies saved outside the App must be managed in the destination service.

## 9. Support communications

Contact Support, Report a Problem and Feature Request prepare an email for you to review and send using your email app. You may include a name, reply address, message, issue details and an attachment. Bug reports may add the app version, operating-system version and device model. Task contents and files are not automatically attached to support messages; a file is included when you select it.

When you send an email, ADS Digital Apps and the relevant email providers receive the message and any attachments. We use them to respond, investigate problems and improve support. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details or unnecessary confidential task information.

## 10. Third parties and this website

The services involved depend on the features you use: Google Play and RevenueCat for subscriptions; Android and its recognition provider for speech; calendar accounts for calendar events; and your selected email, messaging, browser and file-sharing services for requested actions.

This release does not include an ADS advertising network or an ADS cloud task-storage service. We do not sell your personal information. Subscription-service processing still occurs as described above, even though core task data is primarily local.

This legal website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Visiting this website is separate from storing tasks in the App.

## 11. Retention and deletion

Local task information, preferences and attachments generally remain until removed through available App controls, clearing the App's storage, or uninstalling. Deleted items may remain in Recently Deleted until permanently removed through the available controls or the App's retention process. External exports, calendar events and copies in other apps are managed separately. Device backups or transfers, where available, depend on your Android version, device and backup settings; we do not promise that they will recover your data.

**Request personal data deletion:** Use our [Data Deletion Request page](data-deletion.html) to email a request. You do not need an ADS account or the installed app to submit it. The page explains local-only data, support records, subscription information and retention limits.

Support correspondence is retained as reasonably necessary to answer requests, maintain appropriate support records, prevent abuse or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of information you sent us by emailing [adstodolist.support@gmail.com](mailto:adstodolist.support@gmail.com).

You may also use that address to request access, correction or deletion of subscription-related personal information processed for ADS Digital Apps, including through RevenueCat. We may ask for enough information to locate the relevant record and verify the request; do not send your password or full payment-card details. Requests are handled subject to applicable law and any records that must be retained. Google Play may independently retain purchase records under its own requirements.

The App does not create a separate ADS account requiring account deletion. **Deleting app data, requesting data deletion or uninstalling the App does not itself cancel a Google Play subscription.** Manage cancellation separately in Google Play.

## 12. Permissions and privacy choices

You can review and revoke applicable microphone, contacts, calendar and notification permissions in Android settings. Declining an optional permission limits the related feature rather than granting ADS Digital Apps permission to use the data for unrelated purposes. The current App does not intentionally request precise location for location-based reminders.

Depending on applicable law, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us to make a request. We cannot remotely retrieve task information that exists only in your local app storage.

## 13. Security

Locally stored App information relies on Android application storage and device-access controls. RevenueCat uses encrypted transport as described above; external apps and providers apply their own security measures. Do not assume that every local attachment has separate app-level encryption or that every link you choose to open is secure.

Keep your device and accounts protected and avoid keeping the only copy of essential documents in the App. No device, application or communication method can guarantee absolute security.

## 14. Children and international processing

ADS To Do List is a general productivity application and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not provide an ADS account system for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so we can review and address it in accordance with applicable law.

ADS Digital Apps operates from Trinidad and Tobago. Providers involved in subscriptions, speech recognition, calendars, email or website hosting may process information in other countries. Applicable data-protection obligations continue to apply to information processed for the App.

## 15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App, its services or legal requirements change. The Last Updated date identifies the revision. Where required, we will provide additional notice or seek consent for a material change before applying it. A policy update does not authorise undisclosed new collection by itself.
