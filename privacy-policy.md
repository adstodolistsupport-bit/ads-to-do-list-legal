# Privacy Policy for ADS To Do List

**Effective Date: September 23, 2026**  
**Last Updated: September 23, 2026**

ADS Digital Apps ("we", "our", or "us") provides the ADS To Do List mobile application ("the App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use ADS To Do List.

## 1. Information Stored on Your Device

ADS To Do List is designed primarily as a locally stored productivity application.

Information you create in the App may include:

- Tasks and task descriptions
- Due dates and times
- Reminder settings
- Repeat settings
- Completed-task information
- Focus Timer activity
- Productivity and streak information
- App preferences and settings
- Files and attachments that you choose to add to tasks

This information is generally stored locally on your device.

ADS Digital Apps does not operate a cloud service for synchronizing your task content in this version of the App.

Because this information is stored locally, uninstalling the App, clearing its application data, losing your device, or otherwise deleting local application files may result in permanent loss of your information.

## 2. Attachments and Files

ADS To Do List allows eligible users to select and attach files, documents, or images to tasks.

The App accesses files only when you choose to select or attach them using the available file-selection features.

Task attachments are stored locally for use by the App. ADS Digital Apps does not automatically upload your task attachments to an ADS Digital Apps cloud server.

If you choose to open, share, save, or send an attachment using another application or service, that third party may process the file according to its own privacy practices.

## 3. Notifications and Reminders

ADS To Do List may request permission to send notifications so that task reminders and related productivity features can function.

Reminder information may be scheduled on your device for the purpose of delivering notifications at the times you select.

You may control notification permissions through your device settings.

## 4. ADS Pro and Purchases

ADS To Do List may offer optional paid features through ADS Pro subscriptions.

On Android, purchases and subscription payments are processed through Google Play. ADS Digital Apps does not directly receive or store your complete payment-card information.

The App may use RevenueCat to help manage subscription status, purchases, and access to ADS Pro features.

Information associated with subscription processing may include:

- Purchase and subscription status
- Product or subscription identifiers
- Transaction-related identifiers
- App user or entitlement identifiers
- Information necessary to verify access to paid features

Google Play and RevenueCat may process information in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

## 5. Support Communications

If you choose to use the App's Contact Support, Report a Problem, or Feature Request features, the App may prepare an email using your device's email application.

Information you choose to include may contain:

- Your name
- Your reply email address
- Your message or request
- Problem descriptions
- Feature requests
- Files or screenshots you choose to attach
- Limited non-sensitive diagnostic information included for troubleshooting

Support communications are sent only when you choose to send them.

Please avoid including passwords, financial information, confidential task contents, or other sensitive personal information in support messages unless it is necessary for your request.

Support communications may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain support records, prevent abuse, or meet applicable legal obligations.

## 6. Permissions

Depending on the features you use and your device, ADS To Do List may request access necessary for functions such as:

- Notifications
- Files or documents you choose to attach
- Images you choose to select
- Opening or sharing files with compatible applications

Permissions are used to provide the feature for which they are requested.

You can manage applicable permissions through your device settings.

## 7. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

ADS To Do List does not intentionally require access to your precise location for its current features.

The App is not designed to automatically collect the contents of your personal tasks and transmit them to ADS Digital Apps.

ADS Digital Apps does not sell your personal information.

## 8. Third-Party Services

ADS To Do List may rely on third-party platforms or services for certain functionality, including:

- Google Play for application distribution, purchases, and subscriptions
- RevenueCat for subscription and entitlement management
- Your device operating system and compatible applications for notifications, files, sharing, and email functionality

These third parties operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

Task information, attachments, preferences, and other locally stored App information generally remain on your device until you delete the information, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.

Support communications may remain in email systems and support records as reasonably necessary to provide support and maintain appropriate records.

Purchase and subscription records may be retained by Google Play, RevenueCat, or other applicable payment-service providers according to their respective policies and legal obligations.

## 10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures when designing ADS To Do List to limit unnecessary collection and transmission of information.

However, no device, application, storage system, or electronic communication method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate security for your device and accounts.

## 11. Children's Privacy

ADS To Do List is a general productivity application and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly design the App to collect personal information from children through an ADS Digital Apps account system.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to ADS Digital Apps through a support communication, please contact us so that we can review the request and take appropriate action.

## 12. International Users

ADS Digital Apps operates from Trinidad and Tobago.

If you contact us or use third-party services connected with the App, information handled by those services may be processed in countries other than your own, subject to the applicable service provider's practices and legal requirements.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its features, third-party services, or applicable requirements change.

When material changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be revised.
